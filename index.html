Why sponsor us

Small club.
Real, organic reach.

No bots, no paid growth — every one of our 300 Instagram followers is a real UTM student who found us on their own, and our content reaches far beyond them: production-quality event recap videos, months with 11K+ views, and 300+ link clicks driving event signups. Brands have already reached out to us directly. Season 2 means bigger flagship events, socials every two weeks, and cross-university collabs across Ontario.

Audience

A genuinely diverse, ~99% student community — beginner-friendly by design, with people trying padel for the first time at nearly every event.

Competitive credibility

8 players competing in the Canadian Universities Padel League (CUPL), Canada's officially recognized university padel league. Our President also serves as CUPL's Head of Operations for Toronto.

Growth story

Founded October 2025. Season 2 brings a bigger flagship event roughly every two months, socials every two weeks, and a rebuilt competitive roster heading into CUPL.
