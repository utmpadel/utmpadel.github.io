Season 2

What’s on the calendar

Windows, not invented days. Exact dates and venues lock as we book them — this is the inventory a sponsor can plan around.

  1. Late September 2026

    Campus social

    A social before the end of this month. First-timers next to returning players — campus reach, not a closed competitive room.

  2. Early October 2026

    Competitive trials

    Trials for the CUPL roster: 3 men’s pairs and 1 women’s pair. This is where the competitive side of the club is named.

  3. November–December 2026

    Flagship event

    A bigger club event in this window. Date still to be confirmed — the flagship of the season, not a weekly social.

  4. Season-long

    CUPL league play

    Ongoing league games in Canada’s PAC-recognized university padel league. Repeat inventory, not a one-off fixture list.

Why sponsor us

First-timers.
Campus mix.
A CUPL pathway.

Every social brings new faces — students picking up a racket for the first time, next to people who came back. The room is all students, with a good gender split. Season 2 is campus socials, competitive trials, a flagship later in the term, and CUPL league play.

Audience

A mix of new faces and returning players. Good gender split. All students. Lots of first-timers — beginner-friendly by design.

Competitive credibility

8 players competing in CUPL, recognized by the Padel Association of Canada in May 2026. Our President also serves as CUPL’s Head of Operations for Toronto. PAC recognition

Growth story

Founded October 2025. Season 2 is the build year: more court time, a rebuilt competitive roster, and a clearer path from first session to CUPL.
